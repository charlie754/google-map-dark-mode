Google Map Dark Mode: show the latest GOAT Cam banner?

The Maps panel of Google Map Dark Mode shows a small banner for GOAT Cam, the developer's iPhone app. To show the current banner, the add-on downloads it from goatcam.dagoat.io at most every 6 hours. That request carries no cookies, no identifier and nothing about the maps you view; the server sees only that a copy of the add-on asked for the banner, plus your IP address, as with any download.

Firefox files this under technical and interaction data, so it asks you first. If you choose Not now, nothing is downloaded and the panel keeps showing the built-in banner. Dark mode works exactly the same either way, and you can change this later in the add-on's popup, its settings, or about:addons → Permissions and data.
